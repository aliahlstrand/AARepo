How it works

From zero to paged in under five minutes

  1. 01

    Add what matters

    Paste a URL, an API endpoint or a TCP port. Keyword and SSL checks are one toggle away.

    $ beaconly add https://api.acme.dev/health
  2. 02

    Wire up your jobs

    Add one curl to the end of each cron job or queue worker. If a heartbeat doesn't arrive on time, we notice.

    $ curl -fsS hb.beaconly.dev/7f3a…
  3. 03

    Route the right people

    Sync on-call rotations from your calendar and choose escalation steps. Alerts go to Slack, SMS, phone or webhook.

    → slack #incidents · sms @oncall · 5m → @lead

Features

Everything on-call needs, and nothing it doesn't

Multi-region confirmation

A failure only counts once three regions agree, so a single flaky network path won't wake anyone up.

Cron & heartbeat checks

Catch jobs that fail quietly: backups that never ran, queues that stalled, invoices that were never sent.

On-call & escalation

Rotations, overrides and escalation policies are built in. No separate paging tool to pay for.

Public status pages

Status pages on your own domain update from real check data, with incident notes your customers can subscribe to.

SSL & domain expiry

Get a warning 30, 14 and 3 days before a certificate or domain lapses, well before browsers start showing errors.

Monitoring as code

Define monitors in YAML or Terraform, review them in pull requests and deploy them with your stack.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How is Beaconly different from a basic ping service?

Ping services tell you something failed. Beaconly confirms the failure from several regions, works out who is on call, escalates if nobody responds, and keeps your status page up to date along the way.

Can I import monitors from another tool?

Yes. The CLI can import monitors from a CSV export, or you can define them as code in YAML or Terraform.

What happens when I go over my monitor limit?

Nothing stops working. We'll email you and suggest an upgrade, and existing monitors keep running.

Where are your probes located?

We run 18 regions across North America, South America, Europe, Africa, Asia and Oceania. You can pin monitors to specific regions for data-residency needs.
